### 7:30 AM · Reel · R2_afib_cardioversion_200J.mp4
**Caption**
Still starting AFib cardioversion at 120 J? AHA 2025 changed the number.

For synchronized cardioversion of AFib with a biphasic defibrillator, an initial energy of at least 200 J is now reasonable, escalating if it fails. For atrial flutter, 200 J may be reasonable too. Why: 3,000+ patients across trials, with >90% cumulative success at 200 J on all 3 biphasic platforms.

Send this to the person who still says "start low." 😅

This rhythm twists. 🌀 Torsades de pointes is polymorphic VT with a long QT.

Pulse present: magnesium 1–2 g IV. Pulseless: defibrillate (unsynchronized), CPR, then magnesium. After that, find why the QT is long: meds, low K+, low Mg.

**Caption**
Adenosine "didn't work"? Check the push. 💉

It lasts under 10 seconds in the blood. Use a big proximal IV with the arm up. Push 6 mg as fast as you can, then immediately flush 20 mL. Warn the patient and record the strip. No conversion in 1–2 min: 12 mg. Don't give it for irregular wide-complex tachycardia.

### 7:30 AM · Reel · R6_pacing_capture.mp4
**Caption**
Pacer spikes don't mean capture. ⚡

Electrical capture means every spike is followed by a wide QRS and T wave. Mechanical capture means a femoral pulse that matches the paced rate. Skip the carotid, because pacing makes the muscles twitch. Then set the output just above threshold and give analgesia or sedation.

Tag the nurse who always checks the femoral. 👇
