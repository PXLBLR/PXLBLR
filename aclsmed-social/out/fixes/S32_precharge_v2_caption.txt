Pre-charge the defibrillator during compressions, just before the rhythm check. ⚡

Then pause, look and shock in under 10 s. Not shockable? Disarm and resume CPR. Shorter pauses, better outcomes.

Educational only. Follow AHA 2025 guidelines and your institution's protocols.
