Type 1 Brugada pattern: coved ST elevation of 2 mm or more with an inverted T wave in V1–V2 on a 12-lead. Rule out mimics like RBBB, anteroseptal STEMI, hyperkalemia and sodium-channel blocker drugs.

It's an inherited sodium-channel disorder with risk of VF and sudden death, often in young people, at night or with fever. Syncope + this ECG = cardiology now.

Educational only. Follow AHA 2025 guidelines and your institution's protocols.
