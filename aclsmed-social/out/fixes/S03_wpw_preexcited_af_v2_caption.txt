Updated version: we've added IV amiodarone to the avoid list.

Short PR + delta wave = WPW pattern. Put AFib on top of it and you get irregular, very fast, wide complexes.

Avoid adenosine, beta-blockers, diltiazem/verapamil, digoxin and IV amiodarone; they can push the rhythm into VF. Unstable → synchronized cardioversion. Stable → procainamide or ibutilide + expert consult.

Educational only. Follow AHA 2025 guidelines and your institution's protocols.
