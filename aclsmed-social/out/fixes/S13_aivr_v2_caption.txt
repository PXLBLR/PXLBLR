Wide, regular rhythm at 50–110 right after reperfusion? That's likely AIVR, accelerated idioventricular rhythm.

Usually benign and self-limiting. Suppressing it with antiarrhythmics can remove the only escape rhythm. If it causes symptoms (lost atrial kick), the fix is to speed up the sinus rate, not to suppress the ventricle.

Educational only. Follow AHA 2025 guidelines and your institution's protocols.
