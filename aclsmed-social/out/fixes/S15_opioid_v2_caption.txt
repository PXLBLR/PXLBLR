Opioid overdose: pulse but not breathing → ventilate with a bag-mask and give naloxone. No pulse → CPR first; naloxone may be reasonable if it doesn't interrupt CPR.

Naloxone treats breathing, not cardiac arrest. Watch for re-sedation after reversal.

Educational only. Follow AHA 2025 guidelines and your institution's protocols.
