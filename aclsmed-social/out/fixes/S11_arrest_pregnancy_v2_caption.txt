Cardiac arrest in pregnancy (AHA 2025): continuous manual left uterine displacement when the uterus is at or above the umbilicus, and standard ACLS drugs, doses and shocks.

Preparation for resuscitative delivery starts at the recognition of arrest, with the goal of completing delivery by 5 minutes. Call OB and neonatal early; ECPR may be reasonable where available.

Educational only. Follow AHA 2025 guidelines and your institution's protocols.
