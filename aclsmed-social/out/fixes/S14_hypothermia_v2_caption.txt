That hump right after the QRS is an Osborn (J) wave, a classic sign of hypothermia. 🧊

Expect bradycardia, long intervals and shivering artifact, then AFib and VF as it gets colder. In arrest: AHA recommends standard ACLS with active rewarming and ECMO where available; some protocols (e.g. ERC) limit shocks and epinephrine below 30 °C.

Educational only. Follow AHA 2025 guidelines and your institution's protocols.
