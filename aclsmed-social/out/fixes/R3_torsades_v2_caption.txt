Updated version: unstable torsades gets defibrillated even with a pulse.

Torsades de pointes is polymorphic VT with a long QT. Unstable or pulseless → defibrillation dose, not synchronized (AHA 2025). Stable with a pulse → magnesium 1–2 g IV. Then stop QT-prolonging drugs and correct K+ and Mg.

Educational only. Follow AHA 2025 guidelines and your institution's protocols.
