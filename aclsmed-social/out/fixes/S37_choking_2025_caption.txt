AHA 2025 changed choking care: for a conscious adult with severe choking, give 5 back blows, then 5 abdominal thrusts, and repeat until the object comes out or they become unresponsive. 🫁

Can they cough or speak? Let them cough and watch closely. Late pregnancy or can't reach around them? Chest thrusts instead. Unresponsive? Start CPR. Infants: 5 back blows + 5 chest thrusts, never abdominal thrusts.

Educational only. Follow AHA 2025 guidelines and your institution's protocols.
