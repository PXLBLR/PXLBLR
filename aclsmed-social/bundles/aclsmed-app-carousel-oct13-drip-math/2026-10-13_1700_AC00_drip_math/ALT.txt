Six-slide carousel from ACLSMED. Slide 1: 'Drip math in 3 steps' with the formula mL/h = mcg/min × 60 ÷ mcg/mL and three step cards. Slide 2: step 1, an example bag of epinephrine 4 mg in 250 mL converted to 4,000 mcg and then 16 mcg/mL, labelled as an example concentration. Slide 3: steps 2 and 3, 2 mcg/min times 60 is 120 mcg/h, divided by 16 is 7.5 mL/h, checked backwards, with the AHA 2025 bradycardia range of 2 to 10 mcg/min if atropine is ineffective. Slide 4: the weight-based formula with a dopamine example, 5 mcg/kg/min for 80 kg from a 1,600 mcg/mL bag is 15 mL/h, with a warning never to multiply a mcg/min order by weight. Slide 5: the IVmed pump confirming epinephrine 1 mg/250 mL at 2.00 mcg/min and 30.0 mL/hr, with three steps to try it. Slide 6: comment SIM to get the ACLSMED Sim link.
