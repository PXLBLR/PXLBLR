- AHA 2025 Adult Bradycardia With a Pulse algorithm (epinephrine infusion 2–10 mcg/min, titrate to response; dopamine infusion 5–20 mcg/kg/min; atropine 1 mg, repeat every 3–5 min, max 3 mg) — https://cpr.heart.org/-/media/CPR-Files/CPR-Guidelines-Files/2025-Algorithms/Algorithm-ACLS-Bradycardia-250514.pdf (text of the AHA PDF confirmed via search index; direct download returns 403 from this environment) · Part 9: Adult Advanced Life Support, Circulation 2025;152(suppl 2), doi:10.1161/CIR.0000000000001376
- Infusion rate arithmetic: mL/h = dose (mcg/min) × 60 ÷ concentration (mcg/mL); weight-based mL/h = mcg/kg/min × kg × 60 ÷ mcg/mL; 1 mg = 1,000 mcg — standard dimensional analysis, identical to IVmed's own dose-to-rate code (aclsmed/iv.html commitField() and _polarDoseToRate(): 'mcg/min' → (dose × 60) / conc; 'mcg/kg/min' → (dose × w × 60) / conc). Worked numbers checked: 4,000/250 = 16; 2×60/16 = 7.5; 7.5×16/60 = 2; 1,000/250 = 4; 120/4 = 30; 400,000/250 = 1,600; 5×80×60/1,600 = 15.
- Example concentrations are labelled as examples on every slide (4 mg/250 mL epinephrine; 400 mg/250 mL dopamine); institution's standard concentration + smart-pump library + independent double-check per policy.
