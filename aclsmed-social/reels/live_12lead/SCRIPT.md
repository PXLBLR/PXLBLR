# ACLSMED Sim · live 12-lead on the monitor · narrated reel (draft for approval)

IG Reels + YouTube Shorts, 9:16, about 55 s. Voice: "Mikko (narration) v2" (Prayer Voice.app), same pipeline and
checks as the heart-rhythm reels. Nothing posts until the owner approves the cut and signs off as RN.

## What is on screen
- **App:** ACLSMED Sim 2.8.0 Monitor, iPad Pro 13" simulator, landscape, **Hospital shell** (charcoal housing,
  "ACLSMED HOSPITAL" nameplate). The last monitor reel (monitor_lines, 11-04) used the Console shell.
- **Frame:** the whole iPad in its bezel, about 1000 px wide on the 1080 px frame, never cropped or zoomed.
  To make single leads readable, a **magnifier strip above the iPad** shows the same live footage enlarged
  (for example V2 and V3), with a matching outline on the iPad so viewers can see where it comes from.
  Captions sit under the iPad. Everything stays inside the IG / Shorts safe area (y 250-1500).
- **Real footage only:** one continuous take driven by a DEBUG film rig (lead taps, ALL 12 LEADS, the
  rhythm change, PAUSE, PRINT), timed to the narration. Rates and vitals come from the app's own presets:
  sinus bradycardia 48 (BP 102/64, SpO₂ 97), anterior STEMI 108 (BP 102/65, SpO₂ 92).

## Judgment calls to confirm
- **"STEMI in all leads":** an anterior STEMI raises the ST in V1-V4, I and aVL and pulls it down in III and aVF.
  The script shows that across all 12 leads at once, because the reciprocal change is the point a single-lead
  monitor misses. The app also has inferior STEMI (but at 52 a minute, not a tachycardia).
- **Line 9 comparison:** "the only simulator" is hard to prove and invites a reply from a competitor, so the
  draft says "Most code simulators give you one lead." Say the word if you want it stronger.
