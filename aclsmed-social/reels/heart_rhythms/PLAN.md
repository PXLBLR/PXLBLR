# Heart rhythms series — 10 narrated reels, Nov 9–18, 2026 (one a day)

Format (all 10): 9:16, ~30 s, IG Reels + YouTube Shorts safe (all content inside y 250–1500, 80 px side margins).
The owner's heart cross-section (CardioLens Cardiac Conduction illustration, `public/heart.png`, background keyed out)
shows what the rhythm does to the heart; a synthetic lead II strip runs underneath on monitor paper.
Every reel: normal beat first (~6 s) → the rhythm → what it means → what to do (AHA 2025) → "Practice it on ACLSMED Sim
· ACLSMED.COM" + disclaimer. Voice-over: the owner's "Mikko (narration) v2" voice (Prayer Voice.app, Mac only).
Every reel goes through the clinical review gate (sources, independent audit, RN sign-off) before it is scheduled.
