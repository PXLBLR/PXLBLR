## 10 · third-degree heart block · Wed Nov 18

**Instagram caption**
```
Third-degree heart block: two clocks, no connection. 🫀

No signal gets from the atria to the ventricles, so each fires on its own clock. A slow backup pacemaker below the block keeps the heart going, often at only 20 to 40 a minute. Pacing pads on. If unstable, give atropine, but don't count on it. Pace, or start an epinephrine drip.
```
