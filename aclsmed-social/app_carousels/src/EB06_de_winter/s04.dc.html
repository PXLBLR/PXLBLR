Treat it like a STEMI.

ACC CHEST PAIN PATHWAY 2022
de Winter is a STEMI equivalent. It can reflect proximal LAD occlusion and warrants immediate angiography.
2025 ACC/AHA ACS GUIDELINE
The guideline doesn’t name de Winter, but when ST elevation or an equivalent is on the ECG, triage follows the STEMI pathway: reperfusion, preferably primary PCI.
THE TRAP
It fails the ST-elevation cut-offs, so it gets filed as "just ST depression". Tall T waves + upsloping ST in V1–V6 = think LAD.
Not sure? Repeat the ECG, compare with an old one, and escalate early under your local STEMI protocol.
