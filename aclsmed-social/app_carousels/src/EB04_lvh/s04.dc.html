STEP 3 · THE MIMIC

ST up in V1–V2? Check for LVH.

WHY IT HAPPENS
Opposite a deep S, LVH can lift the ST in V1–V3. Guidelines list LVH among conditions that can mimic or hide a STEMI.
Compare with an old ECG, repeat it, and escalate if you are unsure. LVH does not rule out a STEMI.
