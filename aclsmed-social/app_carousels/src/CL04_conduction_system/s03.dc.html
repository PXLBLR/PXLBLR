STAGE 3 · PURKINJE → VENTRICLES

Purkinje fires the ventricles: the QRS.

QRS 0.09 s
QRS: ventricles
T: ventricles recover
Bundle branches and Purkinje fibers spread the impulse through both ventricles at once. That's why a normal QRS is quick and narrow.
NORMAL HIGHWAY
Narrow QRS
Under 0.12 s: the impulse came down His, the bundle branches and Purkinje.
DETOUR
Wide QRS
0.12 s or more: e.g. a blocked bundle branch, or a beat that started in the ventricle.
