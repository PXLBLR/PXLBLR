RIGHT BUNDLE BRANCH BLOCK

RBBB: rsR′ in V1.

CRITERIA · ADULTS
1QRS ≥120 ms
2rsr′, rsR′ or rSR′ in V1 or V2
3S wave longer than the R, or over 40 ms, in leads I and V6
All three should be present. The right ventricle depolarises late, so the last part of the QRS points right and forward: up in V1, a wide S in I and V6.
