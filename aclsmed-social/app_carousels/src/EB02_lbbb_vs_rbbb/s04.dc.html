LEFT BUNDLE BRANCH BLOCK

LBBB: broad R
in I and V6.

CRITERIA · ADULTS
1QRS ≥120 ms
2Broad, notched or slurred R in I, aVL, V5 and V6
3No q in I, V5 and V6 (a narrow q in aVL can be normal)
4ST and T usually opposite the QRS
V1 shows a deep S with a tiny r or none (rS or QS). LBBB also distorts the ST segment, so judge ischaemia with care.
