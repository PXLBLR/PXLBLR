The drip is where errors hide.

LEARNERS PRACTICE

THE PUMP ANSWERS
It shares the patient with the monitor, so the drip shows up in the vitals
TEACHING POINT
IV epinephrine and IV amiodarone are high-alert medications. Practice the independent double-check your policy calls for, before anyone presses START.
