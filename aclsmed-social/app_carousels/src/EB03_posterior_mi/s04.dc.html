STEP 3 · ACT ON IT

Posterior STEMI is a STEMI.

2025 ACC/AHA ACS GUIDELINE
ST depression in V1–V3 could be an evolving posterior STEMI. When ST elevation or an equivalent is found, follow the STEMI pathway.
4TH UNIVERSAL DEFINITION OF MI
ST depression in V1–V3 may mean posterior (inferobasal) ischaemia, especially with a positive terminal T wave: an ST-elevation equivalent. Confirm with V7–V9.
THE TRAP
Anterior ST depression gets filed as "just ischaemia". Ask: is this the back wall?
Not sure? Record V7–V9, repeat the ECG, and escalate early under your local STEMI protocol.
