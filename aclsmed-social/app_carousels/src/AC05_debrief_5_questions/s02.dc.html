FIRST · SET THE SCENE

Feelings first. Then facts.

Open with the goal: “Let's spend X minutes debriefing. Our goal is to improve how we work together and care for our patients.”
1
REACTIONS
“How are you feeling?”
Let the emotion out before the analysis. A learner who's still rattled won't hear the teaching.
2
DESCRIPTION
“Can you please share a short summary of the case?”
Agree on what happened, then ask: “What was the working diagnosis? Does everyone agree?”
