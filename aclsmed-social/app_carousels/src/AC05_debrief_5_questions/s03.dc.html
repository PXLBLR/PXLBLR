ANALYSIS · THE HEART OF IT

Ask before you tell. Then ask again.

3
SELF-ASSESSMENT
“What aspects were managed well and why? What aspects do you want to change and why?”
4
ADVOCACY + INQUIRY
“I saw [observation], I think [your view]. How do you see it?”
Example: “I saw compressions stop while the IO went in. I was worried about perfusion. What were your thoughts at the time?”
WHY IT WORKS
You share your honest view and you hear their reasoning, so you can fix the thinking behind the action, not just the action.
Sample phrases, adaptedPEARLS Healthcare Debriefing Tool (Bajaj 2018)
