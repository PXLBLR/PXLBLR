STEMI or look-alike?

INFERIOR STEMI · THIS ECG
ST elevation in one territory (II, III, aVF) with reciprocal depression in aVL.
PERICARDITIS
Widespread ST elevation across territories, often with PR depression. No reciprocal depression, except in aVR (and sometimes V1).
EARLY REPOLARIZATION
A notch or slur at the J point, concave ST, no reciprocal change, and it doesn't evolve on repeat ECGs.
Not sure? Repeat the ECG, compare with an old one, and escalate early.
