WEIGHT-BASED · ONE EXTRA STEP

mcg/kg/min? Multiply by kg first.

WEIGHT-BASED FORMULA
mL/h = mcg/kg/min × kg × 60 ÷ mcg/mL
EXAMPLE · DOPAMINE 5 mcg/kg/min · 80 kg · 400 mg in 250 mL
BAG

× 60 ÷

TEACHING POINT
Change the weight and the rate changes with it, the dose doesn't. Confirm the weight is in kg before you multiply.
