Compressions move blood. Breaths bring the oxygen.

SUDDEN CARDIAC ARREST
Usually starts with oxygen still in the blood. Compressions circulate it.
DROWNING ARREST
Starts with the oxygen already gone. Without breaths, there's little to circulate.
SO AHA 2025 SAYS
CPR after drowning means rescue breaths AND chest compressions. Give oxygen as soon as it's available.
