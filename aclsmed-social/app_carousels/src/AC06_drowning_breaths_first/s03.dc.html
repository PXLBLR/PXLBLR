WHAT THE GUIDELINE SAYS

Breaths are part of the CPR, not an extra.

CPR WITH BREATHS
Rescue breaths and chest compressions for anyone in arrest after drowning.
BREATHS FIRST IS AN OPTION
Trained rescuers may start with rescue breaths, then compressions (starting with compressions is also acceptable).
FIRST MEANS AVAILABLE
Mouth-to-mouth, pocket mask or bag-mask. Don't wait for the perfect device.
OXYGEN
Give supplemental oxygen as soon as it's available.
CAN'T GIVE BREATHS?
Unwilling, untrained or unable: compressions alone are reasonable until help arrives.
