A pool deck, a pulse of 40.

1
Load MegaCode #13 Submersion / Drowning. He opens not breathing, with a weak pulse at 40: rescue breaths.
2
Ventilation good? Lift SpO₂ and HR on the Remote. If not, change the rhythm to an arrest rhythm.
3
If he arrests, watch the order: breaths and compressions first, then pads. Check the CPR start in the LOG.
WHY START HERE
He still has a pulse. The 2024 AHA/AAP drowning update cites lower mortality (44% vs 93%) when breathing support interrupts drowning before CPR is needed.
