STEP 2 · SUPPORT, THEN DECIDE

Support first. Then ask again.

SUPPORT
Patent airway and oxygen · positive-pressure ventilation as needed · cardiorespiratory monitor · monitor the pulse
ASK AGAIN
Does the bradycardia persist with cardiopulmonary compromise?
YES → START CPR IF HR UNDER 60/min
Then IV/IO access, epinephrine, and atropine for increased vagal tone or primary AV block
KEEP CHECKING
Pulse check every 2 minutes. No pulse? Switch to the Pediatric Cardiac Arrest Algorithm.
Algorithm boxes 3–8AHA 2025 PALS · Pediatric Bradycardia With a Pulse
