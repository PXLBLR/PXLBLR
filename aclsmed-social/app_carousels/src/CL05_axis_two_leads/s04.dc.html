STEP 3 · THE BORDERLINE CASE

I up, aVF down? Ask lead II.

II up
Axis between 0° and −30°: still normal
II down
Past −30°: left axis deviation
Lead II sits at +60°, so it reads positive for any axis from −30° to +150°. That makes it the tie-breaker at −30°.
Some books put the upper limit at +100°. These slides use the AHA/ACCF/HRS adult range: −30° to +90°.
