STEP 1 · READ THE NET QRS

Up or down? Compare R with Q + S.

Positive
R taller than the Q or S is deep
Negative
S deeper than the R is tall
→
Positive in a lead: the axis points toward that lead. Negative: it points away. Lead I looks from the left, aVF from below.
≈
Up and down about equal? The axis is close to 90° away from that lead.
